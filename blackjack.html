Instructions

Money:

• Choose an open seat to join the table.
• Scroll up or down to change your bet before the round starts.
• Click READY UP to lock in your bet.
• Your bet is taken from your money when you ready up.
• Once all players are ready, the dealer gives everyone two cards.
• On your turn, click HIT to take another card.
• Click STAND to keep your current hand.
• Try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over.
• If your hand goes over 21, you bust and lose the round.
• After all players finish, the dealer reveals their hand.
• If you beat the dealer, you win money.
• If you tie the dealer, your bet is returned.
• If the dealer beats you, you lose your bet.
• After the round ends, the host clicks RESET to start another round.
